## 📌 Topics for Speaking Presentations

### Lesson 1: Sense Organs
- Functions and structure of the five senses
- How senses give us information about the world
- Experiment demonstration related to one sense
- Biblical verse connection

### Lesson 2: Human Body Systems (Locomotor System)
- Parts and functions of the Musculoskeletal System
- Digital model presentation
- Comparison of physical growth with spiritual growth in Christ
- Reflection on spiritual seeds and supplements for growth

### Lesson 3: Reproduction in Human Being
- Characteristics of reproduction in animals
- Changes through life
- Respiratory system model presentation
- Report findings with vocabulary and concepts
- Praise and worship reflection
